# Agents

Facilix is an AI-powered smart factory monitoring platform that turns a factory floorplan into an interactive digital twin. It allows facility managers to place virtual CCTV cameras, IoT sensors, loading bay trackers, gateways, zones, and alert markers on a visual map, then simulate real-time device statuses, environmental readings, security incidents, PPE violations, pest alerts, and logistics issues. The goal is to help teams visualize factory operations, reduce manual monitoring, respond faster to incidents, and prepare for future integration with real AI CCTV and IoT devices.
